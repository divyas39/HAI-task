Things that can help

  • A natural, chatty tone is not proof of a person. AI reviews sound casual and include specific-sounding details like dishes, wait times or staff names.
  • Look at the shape. AI reviews are often tidy: a verdict, the details, a balanced caveat, then a neat closing recommendation.
  • Look at the mess. Typos, run-on sentences, odd punctuation, tangents and very local references lean human.
  • Use the confidence scale honestly. 1 means a pure guess, 5 means certain. Guessing is fine; we want to know when you are unsure.
